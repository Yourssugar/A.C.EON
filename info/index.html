ABOUT THE PROJECT
What A.C.EON is, what we believe, and what this site is for.

01 WHO WE ARE

A.C.EON Studio is a creative collective — a group of people who write music, produce visuals, build experiences, and constantly explore what becomes possible when human imagination is paired with new tools.

We create because we have to. That's the only reason that's ever mattered.

02 ORIGINALITY

Everything here is ours. Original lyrics, original concepts, original sound. We don't imitate, parody, or borrow from others without acknowledgement.

If something we've created resembles work we've never encountered — that's coincidence, not imitation. People sometimes independently arrive at similar ideas. It happens. But the intention is always entirely our own.

03 THIS SITE

This is a place to rest. To listen. To wander through something built with care and find a moment away from the noise — somewhere to listen to music, think your own thoughts, and feel no urgency to be anywhere else.

The main branch of the site is open to everyone via the Core icon on the home page — the blue symbol. Everything there is freely accessible. Just tap and explore.

04 NODE AI & THE ARG

Somewhere across the pages, videos, and signals of this project, codes are hidden. Find one — through curiosity, patience, or careful attention to what others skip past — and you can enter it into the Node AI terminal.

A correct code unlocks a unique NFT token: a generated image created for that exact moment of discovery. Made once. Belonging to whoever finds it first. A small reward for the kind of attention that deserves one.

05 PROJECT STATUS

A.C.EON is actively in development. Everything you see now — the design, the music, the structure — may change, evolve, or be removed entirely as the project grows.

What exists now is a transmission in progress. The estimated completion of the main body of work is early 2027.

IN DEVELOPMENT — ONGOING

06 COPYRIGHT

All materials on this site were created by the A.C.EON Studio team and are protected by copyright. You're welcome to enjoy everything here for yourself — that's what it's for.

Distributing our work without crediting A.C.EON Studio may result in complaints and content removal. Respect creative work the same way you'd want yours respected.

07 YOUR PRIVACY

We minimise data collection wherever possible. There is no registration, no mandatory sign-in, and no tracking — your attention here is your own.

If any data is collected through interactive features or the terminal, it is stored on our own servers and is never shared with or made available to third parties. What happens here stays here.
